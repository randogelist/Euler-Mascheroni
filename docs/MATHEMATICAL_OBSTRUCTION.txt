GAMMA TARGET-622: FIXED-ORDER CHECKER OBSTRUCTION

STATUS: NOT YET LEAN FORMALIZED.
This is a self-contained mathematical argument, with exact rational finite
computations. It is not a claim of a new Lean theorem and is not a claim that
gamma is rational. No prime number theorem is used.

CLAIM
The current checker admits only bounded target values. Therefore an accepted
seed together with a GammaBinetSuccessor is impossible. This obstruction does
not require GammaBinetBoundsSound and does not depend on gamma's arithmetic
nature. A known accepted seed is needed to infer that the global successor
structure is uninhabited; without a seed, its preservation law could be vacuous.

ASSUMPTIONS TAKEN FROM THE ACTUAL CHECKER
The finite coefficient list consists of integers c_1,...,c_X. It has positive
sum A, so at least one coefficient is nonzero. The harmonic readout is the
integer B:
    sum_i c_i H_(2^i-1) = B in Z.
The correction order satisfies 0 <= p <= 6. Write m=p+1, so 1 <= m <= 7.
The remainder radius is
    r = beta_m sum_i |c_i| / 4^(i*m),
with
    beta_1=1/12, beta_2=1/120, beta_3=1/252, beta_4=1/240,
    beta_5=1/132, beta_6=691/32760, beta_7=1/12.
The center is some rational c, and lower=c-r, upper=c+r. A passing packet
with target N satisfies either
    0 < c-r and c+r < 1/(N+1),
or
    c+r < 0 and -(c-r) < 1/(N+1).
Both cases imply
    2r < 1/(N+1) <= 1.                         (1)
Other checker conditions can only reduce the admitted set and are not needed
in this argument.

1. EFFECTIVE SUPPORT AND PRIME DIVISIBILITY
Let L be the largest index with c_L != 0. Padded trailing zeros do not change L.
For L >= 2, put n=2^(L-1). For each prime q with n<q<2n, the term 1/q appears
exactly once in H_(2n-1). Every other term there has denominator prime to q,
because 2q>2n. Every term in each earlier H_(2^i-1), i<L, likewise has
denominator prime to q.

Since the entire weighted harmonic readout is an integer, multiplying by a
common denominator prime to q and then by q, and reducing modulo q, gives
    q divides c_L.
The primes are distinct, so their product divides c_L. Thus
    |c_L| >= P_L,
where
    P_L = product of primes q satisfying 2^(L-1)<q<2^L.
For L=1 this is an empty product, P_1=1, and |c_1|>=1 suffices. In particular,
the prime 2 is NOT included in P_1. For L>=2, replacing the upper strict
endpoint by <=2^L makes no difference because 2^L is composite.

2. A UNIFORM RADIUS LOWER BOUND
All seven beta values are >=1/252. Since m<=7,
    r >= P_L/(252*4^(7L)).                     (2)
In particular, even without divisibility,
    r >= 1/(252*4^(7L)).                       (3)

3. ELEMENTARY PRIMORIAL BOUND
Let Q(t)=product of all primes q<=t. For integer t>=1,
    Q(t) <= 4^t.
A strong induction proves this. For t=2a, all primes a<q<=2a divide
binomial(2a,a), so
    Q(2a) <= Q(a)*binomial(2a,a) <= 4^a*2^(2a)=4^(2a).
For t=2a+1, all primes a+1<q<=2a+1 divide binomial(2a+1,a), so
    Q(2a+1) <= Q(a+1)*binomial(2a+1,a)
              <= 4^(a+1)*2^(2a)=4^(2a+1).
Here binomial(2a+1,a)<=2^(2a), because the two equal central terms together
are at most the sum 2^(2a+1). The cases t=1,2 start the induction.
For real t, applying the integer estimate at floor(t) gives Q(t)<=4^t.

4. ELEMENTARY DYADIC PRIME-PRODUCT LOWER BOUND
For n>=5, set C=binomial(2n,n). Since C is a largest binomial coefficient,
    C >= 4^n/(2n+1).
For every prime q, Legendre's formula gives
    v_q(C)=sum_{k>=1}(floor(2n/q^k)-2 floor(n/q^k)).
Every summand is 0 or 1, so q^(v_q(C))<=2n.
Consequently, the product of prime-power factors with q<=sqrt(2n) is at most
    (2n)^sqrt(2n).
For q>sqrt(2n), the exponent is at most 1. Primes with 2n/3<q<=n have
exponent zero: the two floors are 2 and 1. All remaining factors with q<=n
are bounded by Q(2n/3)<=4^(2n/3). The factors q>n give precisely the shell
product. Thus
    C <= (2n)^sqrt(2n) * 4^(2n/3) * product_(n<q<=2n) q,
and hence
    product_(n<q<=2n) q
      >= 4^(n/3) / ((2n+1)*(2n)^sqrt(2n)).       (4)
The restriction n>=5 ensures sqrt(2n)<=2n/3, so these ranges cover the
factors as stated.

5. ALL EFFECTIVE SUPPORTS L>=12 ARE IMPOSSIBLE
Substitute n=2^(L-1) into (4), use sqrt(2^L)<=2^ceil(L/2), and use
2^L+1<=2^(L+1). This gives
    P_L >= 2^(2^L/3 - L*2^ceil(L/2) - L - 1).
Define
    G(L)=2^L/3 - L*2^ceil(L/2) - 15L - 8.
Direct exact arithmetic gives
    G(12)=1228/3 >0,
    G(13)=2591/3 >0.
Also
    G(L+2)-4G(L)
      = (2L-4)*2^ceil(L/2) + 45L-6 >0 for L>=2.
Two inductions, on even and odd L, therefore prove G(L)>=0 for all L>=12.
It follows that
    P_L >= 2^(14L+7) > 126*4^(7L).
Combining with (2) gives r>1/2, contradicting (1). Consequently every passing
packet has
    L <= 11.                                  (5)

6. BASE UNIVERSAL TARGET BOUND, WITH NO FINITE PRIME TABLE NEEDED
Combining (3), (5), and (1),
    r >= 1/(252*4^77),
    N+1 < 126*4^77.
The latter integer constant is
    2877331348495215120213504514410182194947617193984.
Thus the target cannot be cofinal even if the nominal X contains arbitrarily
many padded zeros and the integer coefficients themselves are unbounded.

7. CONSEQUENCE FOR GammaBinetSuccessor
The structure requires preservation of checker acceptance and
    target(next(w))=target(w)+1.
Starting from any accepted seed, iteration k times gives accepted packets
with target=target(seed)+k. Taking k=126*4^77 contradicts the preceding
universal bound. Therefore:
    existence of an accepted seed implies NOT Nonempty GammaBinetSuccessor.
The supplied target-622 seed is one such seed once its check equality is
kernel-proved. This deduction uses no GammaBinetBoundsSound hypothesis.

8. OPTIONAL SHARPENING USING EXACT FINITE PRIME PRODUCTS
The seven beta values satisfy beta_(m+1)<=4*beta_m for m=1,...,6.
For L>=1, therefore beta_m/4^(Lm) decreases as m runs from 1 to 7.
This improves (2) to
    r >= P_L/(12*4^(7L)),
and any accepted packet must satisfy
    N+1 < 6*4^(7L)/P_L.                        (6)
Exact computations below verify that the lower bound on r exceeds 1/2 for
L=8,9,10,11 as well. Together with the analytic-free elementary argument
for L>=12, this gives L<=7 for every accepted packet.
Among L=1,...,7, exact rational comparison shows that the largest right-hand
side of (6) occurs at L=4 and equals
    432345564227567616/143.
Thus the sharpened necessary bound is
    N+1 < 432345564227567616/143,
or, for natural N,
    N <= 3023395554038933.
This is a safe bound, not a claim that this maximum is attainable or optimal.
The actual other coefficients and other arithmetic clauses may substantially
improve it.

SCOPE OF THE RESULT
This proves an obstruction to this particular checker and successor contract.
It does not show that gamma is rational, does not disprove gamma irrationality,
and does not rule out another representation, an uncapped correction order,
or a different cancellation-aware analytic remainder bound. Removing p<=6
would remove an assumption of this obstruction; it would not itself supply a
successor or prove irrationality.
